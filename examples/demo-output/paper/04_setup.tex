\section{Experimental Setup}\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Data and noise.}
The study is small by design: two datasets, one noise model, two baselines. We use two scikit-learn datasets, digits (multiclass) and breast cancer (binary), with a stratified split into
$1{,}257$\src{n_train_digits} and 398\src{n_train_breast_cancer} training samples. Symmetric noise flips each training label independently with probability 20\%\src{noise_low_pct} or 40\%\src{noise_high_pct}. On digits, a flipped label is drawn uniformly from the other nine classes. The unflipped labels serve as the clean reference, and the clean test split is never corrupted.

\paragraph{Models.}
Logistic regression uses $C=1$\src{logreg_C} on standardized features and trains on the noisy labels. The multilayer perceptron (MLP) is an untuned reference: one hidden layer of
64\src{mlp_hidden} units, at most 500\src{mlp_max_iter} iterations, scikit-learn defaults otherwise (Adam solver, default weight decay, no early stopping), standardized inputs, and it never sees the filtered set. The
out-of-fold filter wraps the same logistic regression with 5\src{filter_folds} folds. We run no other noise-robust method, no clean-label oracle, and no ablation over folds, $C$ or MLP size.

\paragraph{Metrics and statistics.}
We measure accuracy on the clean test split (30\%\src{test_fraction_pct} of each dataset). For the filter we also report precision,
recall, the realized noise rate (the precision a random drop would have), and the recovered share. We run each setting with 5\src{n_seeds} seeds. A seed fixes the split, the noise draw, the folds and the MLP
initialization, and all methods share it, so comparisons are paired; the MLP variance therefore mixes data and initialization. We use two-sided paired $t$-tests over seeds. The trend test is a paired $t$-test on the per-seed gain at the higher noise rate minus the gain at the lower rate. With 5\src{n_seeds} seeds and about a dozen uncorrected tests, the $p$-values are descriptive. Fit time is wall-clock time on an unrecorded machine and is indicative only. Because precision is compared with the realized noise rate, we report no test against a fixed level.
We recomputed every number in this paper from the released results and checked every reference against its bibliographic record with the Scriptorium pipeline~\citep{scriptorium2026}.
