\subsection{Filtering regains accuracy on digits, less so on breast cancer}

The filter's mean is above that of logistic regression on the same noisy labels in every noisy setting (Table~\ref{tab:main}). At 20\%\src{cfg_noise_low_pct} noise it wins back 78\%\src{recovery_dg_20} of the lost accuracy on digits and 66\%\src{recovery_bc_20} on breast cancer, and on digits at 40\%\src{cfg_noise_high_pct} noise it wins back 68\%\src{recovery_dg_40}.
Paired per seed, the gain on digits ranges from 3.7\src{pdmin_dg_20} to 7.4\src{pdmax_dg_20} points at 20\%\src{cfg_noise_low_pct} noise and from 5.6\src{pdmin_dg_40} to 11.9\src{pdmax_dg_40} points at 40\%\src{cfg_noise_high_pct}, positive in all 5\src{n_seeds} seeds.
On breast cancer at 20\%\src{cfg_noise_low_pct} noise it ranges from $-1.2$\src{pdmin_bc_20} to 6.4\src{pdmax_bc_20} points, positive in 4\src{wins_bc_20_filter_over_logreg} of 5\src{n_seeds} seeds, and the recovery of 66\%\src{recovery_bc_20} rests on a loss of only 4.4\src{loss_bc_20_logreg} points.

Breast cancer at 40\%\src{cfg_noise_high_pct} noise is where the claim stops. The paired gain is 2.3\src{pdmin_bc_40} to 5.8\src{pdmax_bc_40} points, positive in all 5\src{n_seeds} seeds, but the mean recovery is only 22\%\src{recovery_bc_40}, the filter ends 12.7\src{gap_bc_40_filter_to_clean} points below clean accuracy, and its seed sd (9.0\src{acc_bc_40_filter_sd} points) exceeds its mean gain (3.6\src{diff_bc_40_filter_minus_logreg} points). With 5\src{n_seeds} seeds we cannot tell this gain from a modest one. This is consistent with the binary case of Sec.~\ref{sec:method}, where the true class is barely the majority label and the out-of-fold model is itself weak (80.4\%\src{acc_bc_40_logreg_mean} test accuracy). The small test and training sets are an alternative explanation that we did not test.

\subsection{Most dropped samples are truly mislabeled}

Precision of the drops is 65\%\src{precision_dg_20_mean} on digits at 20\%\src{cfg_noise_low_pct} noise, 76\%\src{precision_dg_40_mean} on digits at 40\%\src{cfg_noise_high_pct}, 76\%\src{precision_bc_20_mean} on breast cancer at 20\%\src{cfg_noise_low_pct} and 68\%\src{precision_bc_40_mean} at 40\%\src{cfg_noise_high_pct}, and at least 61\%\src{precision_dg_20_min} in every seed. Recall is 98\%\src{recall_dg_20_mean} on digits at both noise rates, but falls to 79\%\src{recall_bc_40_mean} on breast cancer at 40\%\src{cfg_noise_high_pct} (Fig.~\ref{fig:drops}). So roughly a quarter to a third of drops are clean samples, and the filter removes a share of the training set well above the flip rate: 30\%\src{droppedshare_dg_20} at 20\%\src{cfg_noise_low_pct} noise and 50\%\src{droppedshare_dg_40} at 40\%\src{cfg_noise_high_pct} on digits, against realized flip rates of 19.7\%\src{noisy_frac_dg_20_mean} and 39.4\%\src{noisy_frac_dg_40_mean}. The refit therefore sees fewer clean samples, which could hurt small or hard classes; we did not measure per-class drop rates. The lower recall on breast cancer at 40\%\src{cfg_noise_high_pct} is consistent with the weaker recovery there, but we did not test why.

\subsection{An untuned MLP fits the noise instead of removing it}

With clean labels the MLP matches or slightly beats logistic regression, but under noise it collapses, consistent with networks memorizing wrong labels \citep{zhang2017understanding}. The filter beats the MLP in every seed of every noisy setting (5\src{wins_dg_20_filter_over_mlp} of 5\src{n_seeds} seeds in each), by 11.0\src{diff_bc_20_filter_minus_mlp} to 27.7\src{diff_dg_40_filter_minus_mlp} points in mean (Table~\ref{tab:main}, Fig.~\ref{fig:teaser}). This is the weaker comparison: the MLP uses default settings without early stopping or extra regularization, and we did not test logistic regression with a tuned $C$ or the MLP trained on filtered data. So the data do not show that filtering beats a tuned model or that it beats regularization. With clean labels the filter is $-0.74$\src{diff_dg_00_filter_minus_mlp} points relative to the MLP on digits.

\subsection{On clean labels we observe a small cost}

On clean labels the mean accuracy changes by $-0.22$\src{clean_cost_dg} points on digits and $-0.12$\src{clean_cost_bc} points on breast cancer, within the seed sd (Table~\ref{tab:main}). Paired per seed, the change ranges from $-1.3$\src{pdmin_dg_00} to 0.6\src{pdmax_dg_00} points on digits and from $-0.6$\src{pdmin_bc_00} to 0.0\src{pdmax_bc_00} points on breast cancer. The filter is below logistic regression in 2\src{clean_worse_seeds_dg} and 1\src{clean_worse_seeds_bc} of 5\src{n_seeds} seeds. It still discards 3.5\%\src{dropped_frac_dg_00_mean} and 1.9\%\src{dropped_frac_bc_00_mean} of the clean training set. Five seeds cannot distinguish a small cost from none; the worst-seed change is $-1.3$\src{pdmin_dg_00} points on digits.
